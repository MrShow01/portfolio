01 — About

Building mobile products that ship

I'm a Senior Flutter Developer with over five years of experience creating high-quality mobile applications from a single cross-platform codebase.

Across that time I've contributed to more than 50 projects and successfully delivered 5 full-scale products from inception to deployment — among them a governmental application now serving the public on both Google Play and the App Store.

My work spans the whole delivery cycle: shaping app functionality and UI, integrating REST APIs and backend services, wiring up payments, notifications and third-party SDKs, then handling release and store deployment. I'm equally comfortable taking over an existing codebase to fix, refactor and extend it as I am starting from a blank project.

I've also taught Flutter professionally as an instructor, guiding students through building production-grade apps and working effectively in a team — which sharpened how I write code others have to read.
